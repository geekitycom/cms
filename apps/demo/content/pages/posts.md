description: Where the demo's post listing lives, now that a page is the front page.
---

`site.json` names this page as the `postsPage`, so the paginated listing is
here rather than at the site root: the Reading settings, WordPress's own pair
of picks. The page's words are what you are reading, and the posts come under
them, `postsPerPage` at a time.

The front page is the About page, which prints its own words and then the most
recent posts. Nothing moved for a subscriber: the feeds are at `/feed/` and its
siblings, exactly where they were.
