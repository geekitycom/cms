`archive: true` in this page's front matter is the whole of it. One key, which
Eleventy ignores, puts every published post below — newest month first, newest
post first inside a month — and nothing paginates it, because an archive page
is a way of finding one piece of writing rather than a listing to read through.

A draft and a post whose date has not arrived are absent, for the same reason
they are absent from the feed: they are not pages a reader can open.
