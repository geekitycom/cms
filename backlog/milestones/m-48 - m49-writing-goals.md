## Description

Each user can set a writing goal such as 250 words a day or 100,000 words a month, with streak rules such as "one missed day is fine, two are not". Words added in the editor (autosave, save, Micropub) are credited to the writer in a per-user daily ledger. The dashboard shows progress, pace and streaks, and the editor always shows the post's word count and the words written today, including in focus mode. See decision-63.
