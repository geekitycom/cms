## Description

The packaged default theme is a plain, neutral layout. Remake it in the image of the asdo-theme WordPress theme at andrewshell.org: a serif body and sans headings on warm paper, a rust primary colour, one 42rem column, a big site title on the front page and a small home link elsewhere, feed items with an excerpt and Continue reading, a Published line inside the entry, a bio h-card under every post and page, facepiles for likes and boosts, and previous/next post links. Every entry is an h-entry inside an h-feed, every person an h-card, categories are p-category, and the head carries Open Graph and Twitter cards and one JSON-LD graph. Not a copy: Nunjucks over the CMS context, with the theme blocks and partials a site overrides kept. Runs after M14, since that milestone moves the theme into its named form.
