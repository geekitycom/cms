## Description

What shll.me showed the moment it went up: a reader lands on a Geekity site and cannot get anywhere. The one page a new site ships is unlinked, the menu is in a different place on every kind of page, the footer prints one nominated user's personal links as if they were the site's, and somebody signed in to their own admin is asked to type their name, email and website to leave a comment.

None of it is a missing feature. Every part exists and is in the wrong place, in two places, or reachable only by somebody who already knows it is there.

The order matters. TASK-106 leaves one source for the menu, so the rest is not arranging two things that disagree. TASK-105 then puts the menu, the person and the site each in one place. TASK-104 fills in what a new site starts with, which is only writable once the first two decide where things live. TASK-103 and TASK-95 are independent of those three and of each other.
