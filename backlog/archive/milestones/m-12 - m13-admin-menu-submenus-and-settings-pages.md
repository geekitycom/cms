## Description

The admin navigation gains WordPress-style submenus: every top-level section has at least one child, the current section expands to show its children, and the child you land on is marked. The settings screen, twelve headings under one form, becomes a family of pages under Settings. Independent of the other milestones; best run before M12 so its user profile and the removal of the actor settings land in the new layout.
