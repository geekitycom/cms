## Description

The post editor shows every mf2 property at once, which led to mistakes like attaching a photo where a read post needed a cover. Pick the kind first and show only its fields. Reviews (books, movies, TV, albums, places, anything else) replace read posts, which are removed. Mockups: _local/editor-mockups (option A, a-picker.html).
