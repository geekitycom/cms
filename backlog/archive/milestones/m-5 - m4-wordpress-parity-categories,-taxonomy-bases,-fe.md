## Description

What a WordPress site's readers and subscribers expect to keep working: categories alongside tags, configurable tag and category bases (defaulting to WordPress's /tag/ and /category/), RSS 2.0 at /feed/ with Atom at /feed/atom/ and JSON Feed at /feed/json/, per-taxonomy feeds, and redirects for WordPress's ?feed= URLs. Reference site: https://andrewshell.org/
